# Session Retrospective

Use `$session-retrospective` and `$failure-to-harness`.

Summarize:

- what slowed or broke the task
- root cause category
- smallest durable harness improvement
- whether the fix belongs in AGENTS.md, a skill, hook, script, test, subagent, docs, or CI
- verification needed
- human approval needed

Do not promote new rules to block mode without fixtures and human review.
