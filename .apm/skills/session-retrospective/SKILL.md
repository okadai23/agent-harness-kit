---
name: session-retrospective
description: Use this skill after a Codex task, failed run, review cycle, or repeated correction to extract reusable lessons and promote them into the harness.
---

Summarize:

- What went wrong or slowed the task.
- Root cause category.
- Smallest durable fix.
- Whether it belongs in AGENTS.md, skill, hook, script, test, subagent, docs, or CI.
- Verification needed.
- Human approval needed.

Do not record secrets or private user data in retrospective artifacts.
